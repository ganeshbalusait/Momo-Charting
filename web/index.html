What it runs

Four engines, one surface.

01
Multi-timeframe signals
MACD cloud bands, EMA stack, VWAP hold and relative volume resolved across 3m → M in one pass — higher timeframes gate the lower ones, so a 5m entry never fights the 4h trend.
02
OI walls & gamma magnets
Every strike in the 0–31 DTE chain, scored for concentration and distance decay. Call walls, put walls and magnet strikes plotted directly on the chart.
03
Agent monitoring
Agents watch the list continuously and hold fire until an EMA stack, VWAP hold, momentum and one of four approved setups all agree — then paper-execute, never before.
04
Live dual-feed streaming
Schwab/TOS drives the active chart; Alpaca streams minute bars for the whole watchlist in parallel — no polling, no rate-limit stalls.

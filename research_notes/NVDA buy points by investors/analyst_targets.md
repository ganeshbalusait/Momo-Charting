Many firms cut targets or downgraded between Nov 11 and Nov 19, and Q3 FY27 results came out Nov 17, 2026. Most targets older than Nov 11, 2026 are now stale.

## 2. Named analysts' targets, reasoning, and downgrades

### Takeaway
Two waves. Wave 1 (Nov 11-16, 2026) was a reaction to Huang's exit: downgrades from Bernstein, Baird, William Blair, StoneX, and reportedly Oppenheimer and Evercore ISI, plus deep target cuts (Goldman $300 to $220, RBC $330 to $230, Mizuho $250 to $220). Most downgrading firms said fundamentals were intact and cut the valuation multiple instead. Wave 2 (Nov 18-19, after strong Q3 FY27 results): most Buy-rated firms reiterated Buy, many trimming targets into the $200-$280 band.

## 3. EPS estimates and implied multiples

### Takeaway

Bernstein's $200 implies 12.7x, versus about 25x at its earlier $400. Next-12-month P/E fell from 20.8x (end of July) to 16.9x (Oct 30) and 15.4x (Nov 6) before the resignation sell-off.
